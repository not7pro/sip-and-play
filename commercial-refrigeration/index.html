02 / TROPICAL ENGINEERING

Why Tropical Climate Class 4/5 Specification Is Essential in Sri Lanka

Active commercial kitchens in Sri Lanka and the Maldives regularly reach ambient temperatures of 38°C to 43°C with 75%+ relative humidity. Sip & Play specifies ventilated forced-air evaporator systems, high-density cyclopentane polyurethane insulation, anti-condensation door frame heaters, and tropicalized compressors engineered to recover cabinet temperature rapidly during high-frequency dinner service.

04 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Commercial Refrigeration FAQs

What is the difference between commercial Tropical Climate Class 4/5 refrigeration and domestic fridges?

Domestic refrigerators are engineered for air-conditioned rooms (+25°C ambient) with infrequent door openings and fail rapidly on a hot commercial line. Tropical Climate Class 4/5 commercial chillers and freezers use high-displacement compressors, forced-air finned evaporators, 60mm–150mm high-density polyurethane insulation, and anti-sweat door heaters to hold +2°C to +5°C (chillers) or −18°C to −22°C (freezers) in +38°C to +43°C Sri Lankan kitchens.

Does Sip & Play custom-size walk-in cold rooms to fit existing structural rooms?

Yes. Our engineering team conducts on-site laser measurements and fabricates modular cam-lock polyurethane cold room and freezer chambers with either indoor or remote outdoor split condensing units.

When should a restaurant or hotel choose a walk-in cold room over upright reach-in chillers?

Once bulk refrigerated storage needs exceed three to four 4-door upright reach-in cabinets (approximately 4,500–5,500 liters), a modular walk-in cold room with a remote condensing unit delivers higher cubic storage density, lower heat rejection inside the kitchen, and easier roll-in shelving access.

What electrical supply is required for upright chillers vs. walk-in cold rooms?

2-door and 4-door upright reach-in chillers and under-counter counters typically run on standard 220–230V single-phase 13A/16A circuits protected by an inline AVR, while large walk-in cold rooms and 10-to-20-tray blast chillers utilize 380–400V 3-phase power.
