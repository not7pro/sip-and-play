Section 01

Data Controller & Executive Overview

Sip & Play ("we", "us", "our") is committed to safeguarding the commercial privacy, proprietary operational blueprints, and personal data entrusted to us by hospitality operators, hoteliers, executive chefs, developers, and trade partners.

For the purposes of applicable data protection legislation — including the Sri Lanka Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022 (PDPA) and international cross-border frameworks — Sip & Play acts as the primary Data Controller for information processed through our corporate website (www.sipandplay.co), digital Client Portal, and direct engineering consultations.

Section 03

Commercial Confidentiality of Client Blueprints & Concepts

We understand that restaurant layouts, culinary workflow innovations, bespoke kitchen lines, and unannounced hospitality projects represent mission-critical commercial secrets.

Strict Non-Disclosure Commitment: All floor plans, equipment specifications, menu volume projections, and project documents submitted to Sip & Play are treated as strictly confidential commercial assets. We never publish, display, or share client project layouts with external third parties without prior written consent from the client.

Section 04

Lawful Bases of Data Processing

We process data only when permitted by applicable law, relying on the following lawful bases:

  • Performance of a Contract: Processing necessary to prepare engineering proposals, fabricate custom stainless steel equipment, arrange international sea freight, and execute on-site mechanical commissioning.
  • Legal Obligation: Maintaining accounting, tax, customs declarations, and port clearance records in compliance with Sri Lankan, Maldivian, and Thai statutory authorities.
  • Legitimate Interests: Improving our commercial kitchen engineering workflows, protecting network infrastructure against cyber threats, and managing business relationships.
  • Explicit Consent: Where the client elects to receive technical newsletters, product updates, or direct engineering advisories.
Section 05

Data Sharing, Supply Chain & International Transfers

To execute turnkey commercial kitchen fit-outs across Sri Lanka, Maldives, and Thailand, data may be shared with vetted partners under strict contractual data protection agreements:

  • OEM Manufacturing Partners: Engineering schematics and custom dimensional specifications are transmitted to authorized factories, including Hambach's manufacturing headquarters in China, strictly for fabrication purposes.
  • Logistics & Customs Brokers: Consignment addresses and commercial invoices are shared with maritime freight carriers and bonded customs clearing agents for port clearance.
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Digital project data is hosted on enterprise-grade cloud providers (including Supabase) with servers located in SOC 2 Type II compliant facilities featuring AES-256 data encryption at rest.
  • No Sale of Data: Sip & Play does not sell, rent, or trade client data or contact lists to advertising brokers under any circumstances.
Section 06

Security & Encryption Standards

We maintain rigorous technical and organizational safeguards to protect data from unauthorized access, accidental alteration, disclosure, or destruction:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted to and from our website and Client Portal is secured using TLS 1.3 encryption protocols.
  • Role-Based Access Controls (RBAC): Access to client project files and quotations is restricted strictly to authorized project engineers and management personnel.
  • Audit Trails: Administrative activities and client document access within the portal are tracked with immutable security audit logs.

Section 08

Your Rights & Sri Lanka PDPA Compliance

Under the Sri Lanka Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA No. 9 of 2022) and international privacy laws, clients and data subjects enjoy specific statutory rights:

  • Right of Access: You may request a copy of the personal and project information we hold concerning your account.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request prompt correction of inaccurate or incomplete corporate or contact information.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request deletion of non-essential personal data, subject to statutory tax and warranty obligations.
  • Right to Restrict or Object: You may object to the processing of your data for direct marketing or request restrictions on specific processing activities.
  • Right to Data Portability: You may request your project specifications and equipment schedules in a structured, machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, please submit a written request to our Data Protection Officer at privacy@sipandplay.lk. We will respond within twenty-one (21) calendar days.

Section 09

Cookie Policy & Tracking Technologies

Our digital platforms use minimal, privacy-respecting cookies and local storage tokens:

  • Essential Functional Cookies: Necessary to store your preferred visual theme (e.g. Light/Dark mode state) and maintain active Client Portal login sessions.
  • Performance Telemetry: Aggregated, anonymized page view statistics used solely to optimize website load speeds and user experience.
  • No Third-Party Advertising Trackers: We do not deploy cross-site behavioral tracking cookies or third-party marketing pixels on our consultation platform.
