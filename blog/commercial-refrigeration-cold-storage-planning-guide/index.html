Section 01

Why European Climate-Class-3 Fridges Fail in Sri Lankan Kitchens

Light-duty commercial refrigerators rated for ISO Climate Class 3 are engineered for a maximum ambient room temperature of 25°C at 60% relative humidity. Inside an active commercial kitchen in Colombo, Galle, or Malé—especially opposite a gas wok or charbroiler line—ambient temperatures routinely reach 38°C to 43°C with 80% humidity, and doors are opened 60+ times per service.

For this reason, Sip & Play's commercial refrigeration systems are specified for Tropical Climate Class 4 and Class 5 (+40°C to +43°C ambient) with high-density cyclopentane polyurethane (PU) insulation (60mm–100mm thickness), heated anti-condensation door perimeters, and heavy-duty hermetic compressors.

Section 03

Ventilated Forced-Air vs. Static Cooling & Sizing Cold Rooms

In high-frequency restaurant service, ventilated (forced-air fan) evaporator systems are essential because they recover cabinet temperature within 90 seconds of a chef closing the door and eliminate warm dead-spots across upper and lower GN shelves.

When deciding between modular walk-in cold rooms (such as the automated refrigeration lines installed at our 33,000 sq ft Chinese Mega Kitchen) and 4-door upright cabinets, read our dedicated engineering comparison: Walk-In Cold Rooms vs. Upright Reach-In Chillers: A Sizing & ROI Guide.

Section 04

Why Never to Put Hot Food Directly into a Holding Chiller

Standard walk-in and upright chillers are engineered to hold pre-chilled food at +2°C, not to remove thermal energy from 85°C stocks, curries, or roasted meats. Placing hot gastronorm trays into a holding fridge fills the cabinet with steam, freezes the evaporator coil solid with ice, spoils adjacent dairy/produce, and burns out the compressor. High-volume kitchens pair their combi ovens with a dedicated commercial blast chiller to drop core temperatures to +3°C in under 90 minutes before transferring pans to holding storage.

Section 05

Protecting Your 24-Month Compressor Warranty

Under Clause 07 of Sip & Play's Master Terms, commercial refrigeration carries a 12-Month Parts & Labor Warranty and a 24-Month Compressor Warranty. To maximize compressor lifespan and keep warranty coverage active:

  • Monthly Condenser Fin Brushing: Kitchen airborne grease coats condenser fins like a thermal blanket. Brush and vacuum condenser coils every 30 days.
  • 150mm Airflow Clearance: Never push upright chillers flush against an unventilated back wall or stack cardboard boxes on top of top-mounted monoblock compressors.
  • Voltage Surge & Brownout Protection: Connect refrigeration lines through an Automatic Voltage Regulator (AVR) with a 3-minute compressor restart time-delay relay.

Section 06

Frequently Asked Questions: Commercial Refrigeration

Should walk-in cold room condensing units be installed indoors or outdoors?

For walk-in cold rooms, installing remote split condensing units on an outdoor shaded, well-ventilated terrace keeps compressor noise and rejected heat out of the kitchen interior, reducing kitchen HVAC load and lowering refrigeration power consumption by up to 25%.

Why do commercial chillers use Gastronorm (GN 1/1 or GN 2/1) shelf dimensions?

GN 2/1 (650×530mm) and GN 1/1 (530×325mm) internal shelving allows chefs to slide standardized stainless steel pans directly from the prep counter into the upright chiller, blast chiller, or combi oven without transferring food between containers.
