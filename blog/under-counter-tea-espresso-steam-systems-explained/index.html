Section 01

Why Modern Hospitality Bars Are Moving Boilers Beneath the Counter

Traditional bulky tabletop boilers block customer sightlines, radiate heat into the barista's working zone, and consume valuable counter real estate. By relocating the heavy copper and stainless steel boilers into an under-counter cabinet while leaving only sculpted chrome group towers and steam pillars above the stone slab, cafés and hotel lounges achieve an open, theatrical bar aesthetic.

As part of our Commercial Espresso & Beverage Systems ecosystem, Sip & Play is the authorized distributor for T&Z (Dazheng Coffee Group) and LEHEHE under-counter platforms across Sri Lanka, the Maldives, and Thailand.

Section 02

T&Z Galaxy VK-1 & VK-2: 6-Stage Segmented Pulse Tea Extraction

In Sri Lanka—the home of Ceylon tea—and across high-volume bubble tea and specialty tea lounges in Thailand, steeping tea manually in jugs results in oxidized, inconsistent batches. The T&Z VK-1 (Single-Head) and T&Z VK-2 (Dual-Head) revolutionize fresh-leaf tea extraction:

  • 6-Stage Segmented Pulse Brewing: Baristas program exact water temperature, flow volume (with 1-gram precision), and steeping pause intervals across 6 extraction pulses via the 2.8-inch grouphead touchscreen to extract fresh loose-leaf tea in under 60 seconds.
  • Multi-Boiler T3 Thermal Engine: The VK-1 pairs a 1.0L extraction boiler with a 6.6L heat-exchange boiler (3500W), while the dual-head VK-2 features dual 1.0L independent extraction boilers plus an 11.0L steam boiler (6200W) with dual steam wands and a central hot water tap.
  • Flush U8-DECK Integration: Paired with the AISI 304 stainless steel T&Z U8-DECK (830×536×35mm), the extraction towers mount flush into a laser-etched drainage platform with an integrated high-pressure glass rinser.
Section 03

LEHEHE DZ23-2Y & DZ23-1Y: In-Wand Temperature Probe & Auto-Stop Steam

During peak rush hours, milk steaming is the primary bottleneck on an espresso bar. Furthermore, inexperienced staff frequently overheat dairy milk past 70°C (scalding lactose sugars) or scorch oat and soy milks.

The LEHEHE DZ23-2Y (Dual-Head, 3000W, 8.0L boiler) and DZ23-1Y (Single-Head, 2200W, 5.0L boiler) solve this by embedding a high-precision digital temperature probe directly inside the 360° articulating steam wand tip. Baristas select one of 4 programmable temperature preset keys, place the pitcher beneath the wand, and the station automatically cuts off steam the instant the target temperature (e.g., 60°C for dairy or 55°C for plant milk) is reached.

Section 05

Frequently Asked Questions: Under-Counter Beverage Systems

How do you ventilate the cabinet beneath an under-counter boiler?

The under-counter cabinet housing the T&Z or LEHEHE boiler chassis must include front/rear louvered ventilation grilles (minimum 200 sq cm free airflow area) so radiant heat does not accumulate inside the millwork.

Can the T&Z VK-1 and VK-2 extract both tea and espresso?

Yes. Using different portafilter baskets and programmable 6-stage pulse profiles, baristas can store dedicated recipes for black Ceylon tea, green tea, oolong, herbal infusions, and coffee extraction.
