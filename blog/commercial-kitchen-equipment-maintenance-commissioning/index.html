Section 01

What Happens During Professional Wet & Thermal Commissioning

Simply plugging in a combi oven, multi-boiler espresso machine, or walk-in cold room after transport is not commissioning. Under Stage 07 of Sip & Play's 8-Step Execution Roadmap, our engineering team led by Ilham Nayeem executes a 12-point live load test before signing the Handover Certificate:

  • Phase & Voltage Verification: Measuring phase-to-phase (400V) and phase-to-neutral (230V) stability under full kilowatt load and confirming Earth Leakage (RCD) safety.
  • Gas Manifold Pressure & Flame Calibration: Manometer testing of LPG manifold working pressure, adjusting primary air shutters for a clean blue flame cone, and testing flame-failure thermocouples.
  • Water Hardness & Dynamic Pressure Audit: Testing incoming potable water TDS and carbonate hardness (<50 ppm CaCO3) and calibrating 9-bar rotary vane pumps on INNOSONG and T&Z espresso systems.
  • Refrigeration Pull-Down Logging: Recording evaporator superheat, defrost cycle timers, and digital thermostat cut-out accuracy across all chillers and freezers.

Section 03

The 3 Preventable Failures Excluded from Commercial Warranties

As detailed in our Equipment Buying Guide and Terms & Conditions Clause 07, three environmental hazards account for almost all preventable out-of-warranty repairs:

  1. 01. Unfiltered Hard Water (>50 ppm CaCO3): Limescale blocks solenoid valves and coats heating elements, causing element burnout. Always replace or regenerate water softener cartridges on schedule.
  2. 02. Unstabilized Electrical Surges & Brownouts: Operating microprocessor ovens, chillers, or espresso bars without an inline Automatic Voltage Regulator (AVR) exposes PCBs and compressors to grid spikes.
  3. 03. Grease-Blocked Condenser Coils & Hood Filters: Allowing grease and dust to choke refrigeration condenser fins forces the compressor to run at extreme head pressures until thermal overload failure occurs.

Section 04

Frequently Asked Questions: Equipment Maintenance

How do you store an EVEBOT edible ink cartridge overnight so the printhead does not dry out?

At the end of service, gently wipe the printhead nozzle plate with a lint-free moist tissue and snap the cartridge into its protective silicone storage clip so the water-based edible ink remains hydrated.

How often should commercial espresso grouphead gaskets and shower screens be replaced?

In a busy café pulling 150+ shots daily, grouphead silicone/rubber gaskets and dispersion screens should be replaced every 6 to 9 months to maintain a leak-free 9-bar portafilter seal.
