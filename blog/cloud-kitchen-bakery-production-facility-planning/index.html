Section 01

How Cloud Kitchens & Central Commissaries Differ from Dine-In Restaurants

In a traditional restaurant, 65% to 70% of leased square footage is consumed by front-of-house dining tables, guest restrooms, and decorative lobbies. A cloud kitchen (dark kitchen) or central bakery commissary flips that equation: 85% to 95% of the building footprint is dedicated pure culinary production, cold storage, and dispatch packaging.

Section 02

Case Study: Engineering the 2,000 Sq Ft Oh Donuts Facility in Dehiwela

When Oh Donuts engaged Sip & Play to expand their 2,000 sq ft cloud kitchen and central bakery production facility in Dehiwela (project status: Expanding), the primary engineering mandate was designing a linear workflow capable of scaling daily production up to a target capacity of 10,000 donuts per day while maintaining consistent crumb texture, proofing humidity, and frying thermal recovery.

Production Stage Engineered Zoning & Equipment Solution Throughput Impact
1. Bulk Flour & Cold Ingredient Zone Segregated dry ingredient pallets + tropicalized reach-in dairy/butter chillers Zero cross-contamination; rapid batch scaling
2. High-Capacity Mixing & Sheeting Heavy-duty spiral dough mixers, dough sheeters, and precision portioning tables Uniform gluten development without overheating dough
3. Humidity-Controlled Proofing Multi-rack stainless proofer cabinets right next to the thermal line Eliminates skin crusting and guarantees identical rise height
4. Thermal Frying, Cooling & Glazing High-recovery commercial fryers under Type-I SUS304 exhaust + tiered cooling & glazing lines Continuous linear assembly directly into delivery boxes
Section 03

Designing the Packaging & Delivery Rider Handoff Airlock

The biggest bottleneck in urban Colombo cloud kitchens is delivery rider congestion at the kitchen door. An engineered cloud kitchen never allows external delivery drivers to step inside the food production zone. Instead, we design a double-sided pass-through dispatch wall with numbered heated/ambient cubbies, a dedicated packaging carton rack, and an air-curtain external rider waiting bay.

Section 04

Frequently Asked Questions: Cloud Kitchen Planning

How many virtual brands can operate out of a single 1,500–2,500 sq ft cloud kitchen?

By designing modular cooking pods under a shared central Type-I exhaust manifold and shared cold-storage / dishwashing core, a 2,000 sq ft facility can comfortably run 4 to 6 distinct delivery brands with zero cross-traffic interference.

What flooring and drainage are best for a high-output central bakery or cloud kitchen?

Seamless antimicrobial polyurethane resin or heavy-duty quarry tile sloped at 1:100 toward SUS304 perimeter drainage gullies with removable sediment baskets and central grease traps.
