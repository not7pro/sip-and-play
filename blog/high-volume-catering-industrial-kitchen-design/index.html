Section 01

Benchmark Case Study: The 33,000 Sq Ft Chinese Mega Kitchen in Colombo

When meal output scales from 200 covers a night to 6,000+ meals per day, restaurant-grade pans and domestic workflows collapse. In our flagship industrial build—The Chinese Mega Kitchen in Colombo spanning 33,000 sq ft—Sip & Play engineered a custom multi-station culinary infrastructure built around three core industrial systems:

  • Custom Multi-Station Island Cooking Suites: High-BTU commercial wok ranges, heavy-duty stockpot stoves, tilting bratt pans, and multi-deck steam cabinets arranged in parallel thermal islands.
  • Automated Walk-In Refrigeration Corridors: Dedicated receiving dock walk-in cold rooms and deep freezers sized for palletized and trolley-based raw ingredient turnover.
  • Heavy-Duty Exhaust & Make-Up Air Manifolds: High-volume SUS304 exhaust canopies with balanced fresh air replacement engineered to evacuate intense thermal and steam loads continuously.

Section 03

Trolley Corridor Geometry, Floor Trench Drains & Loading Bays

In an industrial kitchen, food moves on wheels—not in a chef's arms. As part of our 2D/3D CAD and MEP engineering scope, industrial facilities are designed with:

  • 1,800mm to 2,200mm Main Arterial Corridors: Allows two GN bakery/banquet trolleys or pallet jacks to pass each other safely without scraping wall corners (protected by SUS304 corner bump guards).
  • Continuous SUS304 Floor Trench Gullies: Recessed 400mm-wide stainless drainage channels with heavy-duty bar grating positioned directly beneath tilting bratt pan spouts and kettle draw-off valves.
  • High-Pressure Washdown Hose Stations & Central Grease Interceptors: Enables rapid end-of-shift sanitation across thousands of square feet of quarry tile or resin flooring.

Section 04

Frequently Asked Questions: Industrial Kitchen Engineering

How do industrial catering kitchens prevent foodborne illness when transporting meals off-site?

Either via a Cook-Chill loop (blast chilling cooked GN pans from +90°C to +3°C within 90 minutes and transporting in refrigerated trucks for on-site regeneration) or via active insulated thermal transport cabinets maintaining hot food strictly above +65°C until serving.

Can Sip & Play custom-fabricate industrial cooking suites and oversized exhaust manifolds?

Yes. Through our direct manufacturing supply chain and custom SUS304 fabrication capabilities, we engineer bespoke island suites, drainage trenches, and ventilation manifolds tailored to your building's structural grid.
