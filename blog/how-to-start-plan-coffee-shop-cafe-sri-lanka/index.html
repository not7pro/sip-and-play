Section 01

Linear Coffee Bar Ergonomics: Customer Queue to Pickup Pass

In a profitable specialty café, the front bar is both the theatrical focal point of your restaurant interior and a high-speed beverage assembly line. The counter layout must follow the natural directional movement of the customer queue:

  1. 01. Refrigerated Pastry & Viennoiserie Showcase + POS Register: Customers view chilled cakes and croissants while waiting to order, driving a 25%–35% impulse food attachment rate before reaching the cashier.
  2. 02. Grinding, Dosing & Tamping Zone: Positioned immediately next to the POS so the barista pulling shots receives tickets in order without crossing paths with the milk-steaming barista.
  3. 03. Multi-Boiler Espresso Extraction Center: Featuring a low-profile machine like the INNOSONG ZS3202 (only 463mm tall) or flush under-counter systems so baristas maintain direct eye contact with guests.
  4. 04. Finishing, Edible Latte Printing & Pickup Zone: Equipped with an EVEBOT EB-FC1 or EB-Pro coffee printer, pitcher rinsers embedded in a T&Z U8-DECK drainage tray, and a dedicated customer pickup ledge.

Section 03

Back-of-House Integration for All-Day Brunch & In-House Baking

In Colombo and coastal Sri Lankan cafés, beverages typically generate 45% of revenue with 75% gross margins, while all-day brunch and fresh pastries drive the remaining 55% of ticket volume. Even a compact 150-to-250 sq ft café BOH kitchen should include:

  • A 4-tray or 5-tray commercial convection oven and holding proofer for baking frozen laminated croissants and Danish pastries on demand.
  • A compact commercial induction line, panini grill, and flat griddle beneath a low-noise SUS304 exhaust hood for eggs benedict, sourdough toasties, and hot brunch plates.
  • A refrigerated saladette prep table with GN pans for rapid sandwich and smoothie bowl assembly.
Section 04

Countertop Cut-Outs, Electrical Loads & Under-Counter Drainage

Before your carpenter or stone mason fabricates the café bar counter, verify the exact millimetric cut-out dimensions for under-counter equipment:

  • Espresso Plumbing & Drain Port: A 50mm–60mm core hole directly beneath the espresso machine chassis for the braided water inlet hose, rotary pump line, and reinforced waste drain tube sloping into a trapped floor gully.
  • T&Z U8-DECK / VK-2 Flush Cut-Out: 810mm × 490mm countertop opening (or 405mm × 500mm for the T&Z VK-1 single-head tea brewer).
  • LEHEHE Under-Counter Steam Tower Cut-Outs: 2 × Ø25mm circular holes for the DZ23-2Y dual-head model or 1 × Ø25mm for the DZ23-1Y.

Frequently Asked Questions: Starting a Café in Sri Lanka

What is the optimal height and depth for a commercial espresso bar counter?

Standard barista ergonomic counter height is 850mm to 900mm from finished floor level, with a minimum countertop depth of 750mm to 800mm to accommodate commercial espresso machines (516mm–595mm deep) plus rear cable/hose clearance and front knock-box space.

Can Sip & Play coordinate both the café interior design and the commercial bar equipment?

Yes. Our Restaurant Interiors & Kitchen Design team integrates Front-of-House architectural aesthetics directly with Back-of-House MEP engineering so your coffee bar looks seamless and operates effortlessly.
