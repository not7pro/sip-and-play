Station 01

Receiving, Cold Storage & Dry Racking Checklist

This checklist complements our Commercial Kitchen Equipment Buying Guide and Commercial Refrigeration Planning Guide. All items below can be sourced directly from our Refrigeration Catalogue:

  • 4-Door or 2-Door Upright Reach-In Chillers (+2°C to +8°C): Ventilated forced-air cooling for daily dairy, sauces, and prepped produce.
  • 4-Door or 2-Door Upright Reach-In Freezers (−18°C to −22°C): For frozen proteins, pastry stock, and bulk inventory (or modular walk-in cold rooms for venues exceeding 100 seats).
  • 4-Tier SUS304 Perforated Storage Racks & Dunnage Racks: Keeps dry sacks of flour, rice, and canned goods minimum 150mm off the floor for pest and hygiene compliance.
  • Heavy-Duty Digital Platform Receiving Scale: Positioned at the delivery entrance next to a stainless decanting workbench.
Station 02

Mise en Place, Butchery & Food Preparation Checklist

Explore our Food Preparation Equipment and SUS304 Stainless Steel Fabrication Guide for detailed specs:

  • Segregated SUS304 Workbenches with Undershelves & Backsplashes: Separate tables designated for raw meat/poultry, raw seafood, vegetable prep, and cold pastry assembly.
  • Single & Double Deep-Bowl SUS304 Prep Sinks: Fitted with heavy-duty commercial faucets and under-sink grease traps.
  • Commercial Food Processors & Vegetable Cutters: High-speed slicing, dicing, and grating machines to reduce manual prep labor.
  • Protein Processing Machinery (Menu-Dependent): Commercial meat mincers, bone cutting saws, bowl cutters, and chamber vacuum packaging sealers for sous-vide portioning.
  • Planetary or Spiral Mixers (10L to 60L): For house-made doughs, batters, sauces, and emulsions.
Station 03

Hot Thermal Cooking Line & Exhaust Canopy Checklist

All hot cooking appliances from our Commercial Cooking Catalogue must be grouped beneath an engineered Type-I exhaust hood:

  • 4-Burner or 6-Burner Heavy-Duty Range with Convection/Static Oven Base: The backbone of Western and fusion sauté stations.
  • High-Pressure Single or Double Wok Range with Rear Water Cooling & Soup Pot: Essential for Sri Lankan, Chinese, and Pan-Asian stir-fry kitchens.
  • Commercial Combi Steamer Oven (6-Tray to 20-Tray GN 1/1): Combines convection heat and live steam for roasting, steaming, and banquet regeneration.
  • Flat / Grooved Griddle & Charbroiler (Lava Rock or Radiant Gas): For steaks, burgers, seafood, and breakfast proteins.
  • Twin-Tank Floor or Countertop Deep Fat Fryer: Separates fish/seafood frying oil from fries and poultry to prevent flavor crossover.
  • Under-Counter 2-Door/3-Door Refrigerated Chef Bases: Holds active service mise en place directly opposite the cooking range.
  • SUS304 Type-I Exhaust Canopy with Baffle Grease Filters & Make-Up Air Plenum: Sized with minimum 150mm overhang on all open appliance edges.
Station 04

Service Pass, Plating & Expedition Checklist

  • Chef's Plating Counter with Enclosed Sliding-Door Hot Cupboard Base: Keeps ceramic dinner plates heated at +55°C to +65°C.
  • Double-Tier SUS304 Pass-Through Overshelf with Overhead Infrared Heat Lamps: Maintains finished dish temperature while awaiting FOH runner pickup.
  • Wall-Mounted Electric or Gas Salamander Grill: For rapid cheese melting, gratin finishing, and plate flashing right at the pass.
  • Countertop Refrigerated Saladette / Garnish Rail (GN 1/4 or GN 1/6 Pans): Keeps microgreens, cold dressings, and finishing garnishes chilled at the pass.
Station 05

Warewashing, Pot Wash & Hygiene Checklist

See our complete Commercial Dishwashing & Warewashing Area Design Guide for layout dimensions:

  • Soiled Dish Inlet Table with Scrap Hole & Overhead Pre-Rinse Spray Gun: Positioned next to the FOH dish drop window.
  • Pass-Through Hood Dishwasher (or Rack Conveyor Machine for 200+ seats): Delivers 55°C wash and 82°C thermal sanitizing rinse cycles in 60–90 seconds.
  • Clean Dish Outlet Table & 5-Tier Drying Racks: Allows racks to air-dry via latent rinse heat without towel wiping.
  • Heavy-Duty 3-Compartment SUS304 Pot-Wash Sink (Wash, Rinse, Sanitize): Sized for large stockpots, wok pans, and GN 1/1 sheet trays.
  • Knee-Operated or Sensor Handwash Basins: Mandatory at kitchen entry points and prep zones.
Station 06

Front-of-House Beverage & Specialty Espresso Bar Checklist

Explore our Authorized Beverage Brands and Commercial Espresso Machine Buying Guide:

  • Multi-Boiler Commercial Espresso Machine: INNOSONG ZS3202 (2-Group, 4200W) or T&Z KT3-2A (2-Group, 6200W) for full cafés; INNOSONG ZS3105 (1-Group, 2400W) for boutique dining passes.
  • On-Demand Burr Espresso Grinder(s) & Knock-Box Drawer: Dedicated grinders for house blend and single-origin beans.
  • Under-Counter Tea & Steam Automation (Optional High-Volume Upgrade): T&Z VK-1 / VK-2 under-counter tea extractor and LEHEHE DZ23-2Y continuous auto-stop milk steamer.
  • Edible Latte Art & Cocktail Foam Printer: EVEBOT EB-FC1 (Full-Color) or EB-Pro for signature branded beverages.
  • Commercial Cubed Ice Maker, Bar Blender with Sound Enclosure & Glasswasher: Complete with multi-stage water filtration and ion-exchange softener (<50 ppm CaCO3).
