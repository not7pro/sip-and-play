Section 01

Commodity Box Traders vs. Turnkey Culinary Engineering Partners

In the Sri Lankan commercial kitchen market, suppliers generally fall into two very different operating models:

  1. Retail Equipment Traders: Sell off-the-shelf machinery from a catalog or warehouse floor without visiting your site, calculating your electrical or exhaust CFM loads, or issuing MEP rough-in schematics. Once the crate is dropped at your curb, utility coordination becomes your problem.
  2. Turnkey Culinary Engineering & Direct-Sourcing Partners (Sip & Play): Take single-point responsibility from initial menu workflow planning and 2D/3D CAD schematics through direct factory QA inspection, custom SUS304 fabrication, on-site rigging, and 12-to-36-month warranty support.
