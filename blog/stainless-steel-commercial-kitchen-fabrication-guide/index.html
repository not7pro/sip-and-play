Section 01

Metallurgical Comparison: AISI 304 (SUS304) vs. 201 & 430 Stainless Steel

To the naked eye in a showroom, freshly polished 201-series stainless steel looks identical to genuine AISI 304 (18/8 austenitic stainless steel). However, once exposed to acidic lime juice, vinegar, salt brines, and coastal humidity in a Sri Lankan or Maldivian kitchen, 201-series steel develops brown rust spots and black weld pitting within six months.

Alloy Grade Chromium / Nickel Content Corrosion & Acid Resistance Approved Commercial Kitchen Use
AISI 304 / SUS304 18% Chromium / 8%–10.5% Nickel Excellent resistance to food acids, salt, steam, and sanitizers Mandatory for food-contact worktops, sink bowls, hoods, and drainage
AISI 430 (Ferritic) 16%–18% Chromium / ~0% Nickel (Magnetic) Moderate resistance in dry zones; corrodes in wet/acidic contact Acceptable only for external non-food appliance side panels or induction cookware bases
AISI 201 (Manganese Substitute) 16% Chromium / 3.5%–5.5% Nickel + Manganese Poor; suffers rapid chloride pitting and rust in humid/coastal kitchens Not Recommended for professional hospitality food contact or sinks
Section 02

Sheet Thickness (1.2mm to 1.5mm) & Sound-Deadened Reinforcement

Budget tables fabricated from thin 0.7mm or 0.8mm sheet metal dent and flex the first time a butcher cleaves bone or places a 60-liter stockpot on the surface. Sip & Play specifies heavy-duty architectural gauges across our equipment and fabrication schedules:

  • 1.5mm (16 Gauge) No. 4 Hairline Brushed SUS304 Top: Used for heavy butchery tables, pot-wash sink bowls, and chef plating counters, reinforced beneath with omega hat-channels and moisture-resistant sound-deadening backing board.
  • 1.2mm (18 Gauge) SUS304 Construction: Standard for general prep worktables, sliding-door cabinets, double overshelves, and Type-I exhaust hood canopies.
  • Ø38mm Tubular SUS304 Legs with Adjustable Stainless Bullet Feet: Ensures rigid load capacity and allows 30mm of leveling adjustment over sloped quarry tile floors.
Section 03

Hygienic Construction: TIG Seam Welding, Coved Bowls & Backsplashes

Health inspectors examine joints and corners because food residue trapped in unsealed seams breeds Listeria and Salmonella:

  • Continuous Argon TIG Welded & Polished Seams: All joints are fully TIG-welded, ground smooth, and grain-matched so no open crevices exist.
  • 100mm–150mm Rear Upturned Backsplash (45° Returned Top Edge): Prevents liquids and food scraps from falling behind tables pushed against tiled walls.
  • Pressed / Coved Sink Bowls with Marine Anti-Spill Edges: Deep 400mm–500mm sink bowls feature rounded internal corners for effortless wipe-down and raised perimeter marine edges to contain water splashes.

Section 05

Frequently Asked Questions: Stainless Steel Fabrication

How can we test on site whether delivered tables are genuine SUS304 vs. 201 steel?

A chemical stainless steel molybdenum/nickel test drop kit (304 identification reagent) turns 201-series high-manganese steel dark brick-red within 60 seconds while leaving genuine AISI 304 unblemished.

What cleaning chemicals should never be used on SUS304 stainless steel?

Never allow raw chlorine bleach, hydrochloric acid tile cleaners, or carbon-steel wire wool pads to sit on stainless steel surfaces, as chloride ions break down the passive chromium-oxide layer. Always clean with warm water, neutral pH food-safe detergent, and microfiber or nylon pads following the brushed grain direction.
