Section 01

Why You Must Size for Your Peak 15-Minute Rush, Not Daily Averages

A restaurant or café selling 90 coffees across a 10-hour day averages only 9 cups per hour—which sounds effortless for any single-group machine. However, if 55 of those 90 cups are ordered between 8:15 AM and 9:15 AM, or immediately after a 40-guest conference break, a single brew group and 1.0-liter steam boiler will create a 20-minute ticket backlog.

Section 03

The Modular Hybrid Alternative: 1-Group Espresso + LEHEHE Under-Counter Steam

What if your counter width is limited to 550mm, yet your menu features high volumes of steamed-milk beverages (cappuccinos, matcha lattes, hot chocolates, and Ceylon milk teas)?

A highly effective engineering solution is pairing a compact 1-Group INNOSONG ZS3105 (2400W) with a LEHEHE DZ23-1Y (2200W, 5.0L boiler) or DZ23-2Y (3000W, 8.0L boiler) Under-Counter Steam Station. This gives your bar massive continuous steam capacity and automatic temperature-probe milk frothing while occupying almost no additional counter width.

Section 04

Frequently Asked Questions: 1-Group vs. 2-Group Espresso

Does the 1-Group INNOSONG ZS3105 use the same commercial rotary pump as the 2-Group ZS3202?

Yes. Unlike domestic vibration-pump machines, the INNOSONG ZS3101, ZS3102, and ZS3105 utilize commercial 9-bar rotary vane pumps and 58mm saturated brew groups for true commercial extraction quality.

Can one barista operate a 2-group machine effectively?

Absolutely. A single skilled barista on a 2-group machine can pull four shots simultaneously across two double portafilters while steaming a large milk pitcher, doubling peak hourly output compared to a single grouphead.
