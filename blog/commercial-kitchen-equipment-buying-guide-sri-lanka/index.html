Section 01

Why Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) Outweighs Upfront Purchase Price

When procuring commercial kitchen equipment in Sri Lanka, comparing quotations purely on sticker price is hazardous. A low-cost reach-in chiller built with thin insulation, non-tropicalized capillary tubes, and ferritic stainless steel will consume 35% more electricity in a 38°C Colombo kitchen and suffer compressor burnout within 14 months. Similarly, importing uncertified grey-market machinery without local spare parts inventory or engineering backing leaves a restaurant crippled when a heating element or control board fails on a Friday night.

At Sip & Play, our supply chain model bridges direct factory sourcing in China (managed by our Supply Chain Specialist Vivian Hu) with local mechanical engineering, installation, and after-sales warranty enforcement (led by Engineer Ilham Nayeem). Always begin procurement only after finalizing your spatial layout—see our Commercial Kitchen Design & Layout Planning Guide.

Section 03

Energy Selection: Bulk LPG Gas vs. 3-Phase Electric vs. Induction

Selecting the right energy matrix in Sri Lanka and the Maldives depends on utility tariff structures, building transformer capacity, and fire safety regulations:

  • Commercial LPG Gas (Open Burners, Woks, Deck Ovens, Fryers): Delivers instant visual flame response and high BTU output independent of electrical grid load limits. Requires an external ventilated cylinder bank with automatic changeover regulators, high/low pressure stages, and solenoid gas interlocks connected to the exhaust hood.
  • Three-Phase Electric (380–400V, 50Hz): Mandatory for combi steamer ovens, high-capacity deck and rotary ovens, spiral dough mixers, hood/conveyor dishwashers, and high-output espresso/tea stations (such as the 6200W T&Z KT3-2A or T&Z VK-2 when wired for balanced phase distribution).
  • Commercial Induction Suites: Preferred in Maldives overwater resort kitchens, basement mall food courts, and luxury hotel open kitchens where naked flames are restricted. Converts 90% of electrical energy directly into the cookware, cutting kitchen radiant heat and exhaust cooling loads dramatically.
Section 04

Direct Factory Sourcing, QA Inspection & Procurement Milestones

Unlike trading middlemen who buy off-the-shelf wholesale stock without quality verification, Sip & Play manages direct factory production and quality assurance. As governed by Clauses 04 & 05 of our Master Commercial Agreement, institutional equipment procurement follows a transparent structure:

  1. Stage 1 — 50% Design & Procurement Deposit: Payable upon contract execution and 2D/3D CAD sign-off to initiate raw SUS304 material allocation and OEM factory production slotting.
  2. Stage 2 — 40% Factory Dispatch & Bill of Lading: Payable upon completion of factory QA inspection, electrical/thermal burn-in testing, and issuance of the marine Bill of Lading prior to vessel departure.
  3. Stage 3 — 10% Handover & Commissioning: Payable immediately upon physical delivery, mechanical positioning, utility hookup, and successful wet/thermal commissioning at your site.

Standard Production & Sea Freight Lead Times: Custom stainless steel fabrication and consolidated ocean container freight typically require 4 to 8 weeks from deposit confirmation to port arrival (Incoterms 2020 CIF Colombo for Sri Lanka, CIF Malé for Maldives, or CIF Bangkok/Phuket for Thailand).

Section 05

Site Readiness Prerequisites Before Equipment Delivery

To ensure smooth installation on delivery day, the client's civil and MEP contractors must prepare the site according to Sip & Play's MEP rough-in drawings (Clause 06):

  • Finished, level non-slip quarry tile or epoxy resin flooring capable of bearing heavy concentrated equipment point loads.
  • Dedicated single-phase (220–230V) and 3-phase (380–400V) electrical isolators terminated within 1.0 meter of each designated equipment footprint.
  • Potable water supply lines delivering a minimum of 2.5 bar dynamic pressure with isolation ball valves within 1.0 meter of wet appliances.
  • Floor drainage gullies, grease traps, and tested LPG gas manifolds terminated within 1.0 meter of appliance connection points.
  • Unobstructed corridors, doorways, and freight elevator access from the unloading bay to the kitchen floor.

Section 07

Frequently Asked Questions: Equipment Procurement

Why are voltage stabilizers and water softeners mandatory for commercial kitchen warranties?

Power grid fluctuations and brownouts can destroy microprocessor PCB boards and compressor windings, while hard water above 50 ppm CaCO3 causes rapid limescale calcification inside espresso boilers, steam stations, and combi oven generators. Installing an inline AVR and water filtration system protects your capital investment and keeps your manufacturer warranty valid.

Can Sip & Play consolidate an entire hotel or restaurant equipment package into a single shipment?

Yes. Through our direct factory supply chain infrastructure, we consolidate cooking suites, refrigeration, bakery machinery, custom SUS304 stainless steel fabrication, and authorized beverage brands (EVEBOT, INNOSONG, T&Z, LEHEHE) into coordinated container shipments for turnkey delivery.
