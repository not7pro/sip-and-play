Section 01

How Commercial Edible Beverage & Food Printing Works

Modern commercial food printers developed by EVEBOT combine optical cup-height auto sensors with precision thermal inkjet heads that deposit food-grade, odorless, tasteless edible ink directly onto milk micro-foam, egg-white cocktail foam, beer heads, macarons, cookies, and fondant.

Guests simply scan a dedicated QR code at the café counter or hotel table using their smartphone browser (no app download required), upload a selfie, corporate logo, or birthday message, and the barista prints the image in 600 DPI resolution in seconds. Section 03

Cartridge Yield, Per-Cup Margin Economics & Food Safety Certifications

Because a single FB3 Full-Color cartridge yields 600 to 800 prints and an FB2 Coffee-Brown cartridge yields 800 to 1,000 prints, the ink cost per cup is only a tiny fraction of the menu upcharge. Cafés and hotel lounges routinely charge a premium for custom printed signature lattes or corporate event welcome drinks, allowing the machine to pay back its capital cost within a single peak season while generating free organic Instagram and TikTok reach.

Certified Edible Ink Safety: All genuine EVEBOT ink cartridges supplied by Sip & Play hold international food safety certifications (FDA / SGS compliant) and are formulated from food-grade water, glycerol, and approved food colorants with a 12-month sealed shelf life (6 months once opened).

Section 04

Barista Best Practices for Crisp 600 DPI Foam Prints

  • Silky Micro-Foam Canvas: Steam milk to a fine, bubble-free glossy micro-foam (using an INNOSONG/T&Z steam wand or automated LEHEHE DZ23-2Y temperature-controlled steamer) and pour flat to within 3mm of the cup rim so the optical sensor positions the printhead accurately.
  • Iced Drinks & Cocktails: For iced lattes, cold brews, or mocktails, top the glass with a 15mm layer of cold milk foam, whipped cream, or aquafaba/egg-white foam to create an opaque white printing canvas.
  • Bakery & Plate Garnishing with PrintPen: Use the handheld EVEBOT PrintPen to roll branded logos across burger buns, cheesecake slices, macarons, and white ceramic plate rims right at the service pass.

Section 05

Frequently Asked Questions: EVEBOT Coffee Printers

Does the edible ink change the taste or aroma of specialty coffee?

No. Genuine EVEBOT edible inks are completely flavorless and odorless, preserving the delicate tasting notes of single-origin espresso and specialty teas.

Which EVEBOT model is best for a busy resort breakfast buffet vs. a high-volume coffee shop?

For luxury hotels, weddings, and experiential cafés wanting full photographic selfies and colorful brand logos, the EVEBOT EB-FC1 is the premier choice. For ultra-high-volume commuter coffee bars needing 5-to-15-second monochromatic latte prints, the EVEBOT EB-Pro is engineered for maximum speed.
