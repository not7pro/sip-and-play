05 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Commercial Kitchen Design FAQs

Who designs commercial kitchens and provides MEP blueprints in Sri Lanka?

Sip & Play (headquartered at 11A/1 Rathnawali Rd, Dehiwela, Colombo) designs commercial kitchens and produces 5-part 2D AutoCAD layouts, 3D visualizations, and electrical, plumbing, gas, and exhaust MEP rough-in blueprints for hotels, restaurants, and commissaries across Sri Lanka, the Maldives, and Thailand.

What is included in a turnkey commercial kitchen engineering package?

A turnkey package includes operational menu and capacity assessment, 2D AutoCAD equipment layouts, 3D spatial modeling, coordinated MEP utility rough-in drawings (1.0m termination tolerance), direct factory equipment procurement, custom AISI 304 stainless steel fabrication, on-site installation, live-fire commissioning, and 12-to-36-month warranty support.

Can we hire Sip & Play for 2D/3D CAD design and MEP blueprints before finalizing our equipment BOQ?

Yes. We execute standalone architectural kitchen design and MEP engineering engagements as well as full turnkey design-and-supply contracts where design milestones integrate directly into your 50% / 40% / 10% equipment procurement schedule.

What regulatory and engineering standards apply to commercial kitchen design in Sri Lanka?

Commercial kitchens in Sri Lanka must comply with the Food Act No. 26 of 1980 (administered by Municipal Council Public Health Inspectors), Central Environmental Authority (CEA) grease-trap and exhaust discharge rules, local Fire Department LPG manifold safety requirements, and international HACCP unidirectional food safety zoning. Safety-critical electrical (CEB/LECO 400V 3-phase), structural, and fire-suppression installations should always be signed off by qualified local MEP engineers.

Do you design hotel, resort, and cloud kitchen facilities outside Colombo?

Yes. We engineer facilities island-wide across Sri Lanka and support resort and hospitality projects in the Maldives and Thailand.
