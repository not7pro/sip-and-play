02 / MODULAR UNDER-COUNTER BAR SYSTEMS

T&Z Under-Counter Tea Brewers & LEHEHE Auto-Stop Steam Stations

High-volume hospitality bars increasingly separate espresso extraction from fresh-leaf tea brewing and milk steaming by installing modular under-counter boiler systems:

  • T&Z VK-1 (Single-Head, 3500W) & VK-2 (Dual-Head, 6200W): Under-counter Multi-Boiler T3 tea extractors with 6-stage segmented pulse brewing (1g water flow precision via 2.8" group touchscreen). Countertop cut-out: 405×500mm (VK-1) or 810×490mm (VK-2 / AISI 304 U8-DECK flush drainage deck).
  • LEHEHE DZ23-2Y (Dual-Head, 3000W, 8.0L) & DZ23-1Y (Single-Head, 2200W, 5.0L): Under-counter continuous steam and hot water towers featuring an in-wand digital temperature probe and 4 programmable temperature preset keys for automatic milk-frothing shutoff.

04 / UTILITY & WARRANTY STANDARDS

Water Softening, Electrical Stabilization & 12-Month Warranty

Under Clauses 06 and 07 of our Terms & Conditions, all commercial espresso and steam platforms carry a 12-Month Standard Commercial Warranty (Parts & On-Site Labor). To prevent boiler calcified scale and PCB surge damage, every installation requires potable water supply at ≥2.5 bar pressure treated through an ion-exchange water softener (maintaining <50 ppm CaCO3 hardness) and an inline Automatic Voltage Regulator (AVR) within 1.0 meter of the machine.
