## 1. Strategic Overview & Honest Forecasting Principles

Sip & Play operates in a high-ticket, low-volume-per-keyword B2B market: **commercial kitchen engineering, MEP planning, custom SUS 304 stainless steel fabrication, commercial refrigeration, bakery production equipment, and specialty coffee systems**.

Unlike consumer e-commerce sites where individual keywords generate tens of thousands of searches per month, B2B commercial foodservice search demand in Sri Lanka is spread across **hundreds of high-intent equipment model searches, category comparison queries, technical MEP specifications, and turnkey project procurement terms**.

### How Cumulative Impressions Scale Authentically
- **No Fabricated Projections:** Because historical Google Search Console (GSC) baseline impression data has just begun collecting, exact monthly traffic numbers are not fabricated. Forecasts must be calibrated after the first **30 days of verified GSC query data**.
- **Multi-Surface Impression Compounding:** Reaching high cumulative organic search impressions over a 12-to-24-month horizon requires:
  1. **36 Indexed Static HTML URLs (Completed Today):** 10 Core/Service/Legal pages + 6 New Static Commercial Category Landing Pages + 20 Long-Form Pillar & Cluster Technical Guides.
  2. **Long-Tail Specification & FAQ Snippets:** Each of the 6 commercial landing pages and 20 engineering guides targets 15–40 semantic long-tail variants (e.g., *SUS 304 vs 201 kitchen table*, *CFM per linear foot exhaust hood Sri Lanka*, *three-phase combi oven electrical load*, *multi-boiler espresso machine price Sri Lanka*).
  3. **Image Search & Schema Rich Results:** High-resolution architectural project renders, `Service`, `CollectionPage`, `BlogPosting`, and `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD schemas expand SERP real estate and image search impressions.
