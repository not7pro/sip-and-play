## 2. Verified Competitor Findings vs. Strategic Hypotheses

### 2.1 Verified Findings (Directly Observed via Public Web Inspection)
1. **Fragmentation Between Engineering Consultants and Box Retailers:**
   - Only **EKTC (`ektc.asia`)**, **Waverley (`waverley.lk`)**, and **BC Kitchen (`bckitchen.lk`)** actively position around turnkey kitchen layout design, exhaust ventilation, and MEP coordination.
   - **Winrich (`winrich.lk`)**, **Chef's Choice (`chefchoicelk.com`)**, **Asparai (`asparai.lk`)**, **Kitchen World (`kitchenworld.lk`)**, and **Seven Waves Smart (`sevenwavesmart.lk`)** operate primarily as product catalog / showroom distributors.
2. **Content Depth Asymmetry:**
   - **Waverley (`waverley.lk`)** is currently the only surveyed local competitor publishing structured educational content mapped to kitchen workflow sections (receiving, cold rooms, prep, cooking line, pass, warewashing, and extraction).
   - None of the 8 competitors publish deep technical guides on **multi-boiler T3 espresso extraction, under-counter segmented pulse tea brewers (T&Z VK-1/VK-2), auto-stop temperature-probe steam stations (LEHEHE DZ23-2Y), or 600 DPI edible beverage printing (EVEBOT)**.
3. **Project Portfolio Proof Gap:**
   - While several competitors list client logos or sector names, very few publish quantified square-footage and daily-output engineering breakdowns comparable to Sip & Play's **33,000 sq ft Chinese Mega Kitchen (6,000 meals/day)**, **2,500 sq ft Mustard Gourmet (2,000 pax)**, and **2,000 sq ft Oh Donuts Facility (10,000 donuts/day)**.

### 2.2 Strategic Hypotheses (To Validate via Google Search Console Over 90 Days)
1. **Hypothesis 1 — Static Category Landing Pages Will Outrank Pure JS Catalogs:** Because `/equipment/` previously relied on client-side JS query strings (`?category=coffee`, `?category=refrigeration`), search crawlers had no dedicated static HTML landing pages for core commercial categories. Launching `/commercial-kitchen-design/`, `/commercial-coffee-machines/`, `/commercial-cooking-equipment/`, `/commercial-refrigeration/`, `/commercial-bakery-equipment/`, and `/stainless-steel-kitchen-fabrication/` provides dedicated indexable URLs to compete directly against `winrich.lk`, `ektc.asia`, and `bckitchen.lk`.
2. **Hypothesis 2 — Technical Specification & MEP Guides Capture Pre-Tender B2B Architects:** Hospitality project managers and MEP engineers search for technical tolerances (CFM formulas, grease trap sizing, SUS304 vs 201 metallurgy, 1-group vs 2-group power amps) 4 to 12 weeks before issuing an equipment tender BOQ.
