Priority is scored on a **100-point Opportunity & Commercial Value Rubric** combining:
  1. **Commercial & Conversion Relevance (0–40 pts):** Alignment with Sip & Play's high-margin turnkey kitchen engineering, equipment procurement, and authorized OEM distribution.
  2. **SERP Content Gap & Authority Advantage (0–30 pts):** Absence of in-depth engineering guides among local Sri Lankan competitors (most local competitors publish thin category pages without CAD/MEP or specification depth).
  3. **Buyer Intent Strength (0–30 pts):** Transactional, commercial investigation, or high-value B2B problem-solving intent.
