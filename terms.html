Clause 01

Scope of Agreement & Acceptance

This Master Commercial Agreement ("Agreement", "Terms") constitutes a legally binding contract between Sip & Play (operating as Sip & Play Commercial Culinary Engineering, registered at 11A/1 Rathnawali Road, Dehiwela, Sri Lanka) and the commercial client, corporate entity, hotelier, restaurateur, or developer ("Client", "Buyer") engaging our services.

By approving an architectural quotation, executing a spatial design contract, submitting a purchase order, accessing our digital Client Portal, or authorizing equipment procurement, the Client explicitly acknowledges that they have read, understood, and consented to these Terms in full.

Commercial Scope: Sip & Play operates exclusively as a B2B (Business-to-Business) and institutional commercial culinary facility engineering consultancy, equipment supplier, and authorized agent for OEM manufacturers including Hambach.

Clause 02

Commercial Kitchen Design, CAD & Engineering Blueprints

Sip & Play produces bespoke architectural layout plans, 2D AutoCAD drawings, 3D BIM spatial models, mechanical-electrical-plumbing (MEP) schematics, and ventilation exhaust ducting calculations.

  • Intellectual Property: All preliminary layout concepts, engineering schematics, and custom fabrication drawings created by Sip & Play remain our exclusive intellectual property until the complete project consultancy fee is paid in full.
  • Client Approval & Sign-off: The Client is responsible for reviewing and formally signing off on dimensional measurements, utility entry points, gas supply lines, drainage trenches, and door clearances before equipment fabrication commences.
  • Revisions: Each design proposal includes up to two (2) complimentary rounds of layout iterations. Subsequent structural or operational revisions requested by the Client after design freeze will be billed at our standard engineering hourly rates.
Clause 03

Equipment Quotations, Pricing & Currency Policy

All commercial equipment schedules, turnkey tenders, and engineering estimates are provided in writing and are subject to the following conditions:

  • Validity Period: Standard quotations remain valid for thirty (30) calendar days from the date of issuance, unless otherwise explicitly noted.
  • Multi-Currency Pricing: Quotes are issued in Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR), United States Dollars (USD), Maldivian Rufiyaa (MVR), or Thai Baht (THB). Where cross-border procurement is involved, exchange rate fluctuations exceeding 3% between quote date and deposit date may be adjusted accordingly.
  • Duties & Tariffs: Unless stated as DDP (Delivered Duty Paid), quotations exclude local import duties, port demurrage, value-added taxes (VAT), social security levies (SSCL), or airport terminal handling fees mandated by the destination jurisdiction.
Clause 04

Procurement Milestones & Payment Schedules

Unless alternative commercial credit arrangements have been confirmed in writing by a director of Sip & Play, standard turnkey kitchen projects adhere to the following milestone schedule:

Milestone Billing Percentage Trigger Event
1. Design & Procurement Deposit 50% of Contract Value Upon contract execution & final CAD blueprint approval
2. Factory Dispatch & Bill of Lading 40% of Contract Value Upon factory QA completion & shipping dispatch / customs clearance
3. Handover & Commissioning 10% of Contract Value Upon physical installation, utility hook-up testing, & staff operational training

Late payments beyond seven (7) banking days from the invoice due date shall accrue commercial financing interest at 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permissible under applicable national law.

Clause 05

Manufacturing, Supply Chain & Freight Logistics

Equipment supplied by Sip & Play is sourced through our direct manufacturing networks, including certified OEM facilities in China and authorized European partners:

  • Lead Times: Standard fabrication and sea freight transit periods range between 4 to 8 weeks from deposit receipt, depending on custom stainless steel fabrication requirements and shipping vessel availability.
  • Inspection & Factory QA: All heavy equipment undergoes pre-shipment testing, insulation resistance checks, and photographic documentation prior to port loading.
  • Shipping Terms: International consignments are governed by Incoterms® 2020 (typically CIF Colombo, CIF Malé, or CIF Bangkok/Phuket unless bespoke door-to-door delivery is contracted).
Clause 06

Site Preparation, Mechanical Installation & Handover

To ensure flawless execution and technician safety, the Client agrees to satisfy the following site readiness prerequisites prior to equipment delivery:

  • Utility Connections: The Client is responsible for bringing dedicated single-phase / 3-phase electrical power isolators, incoming cold/hot potable water supply lines (minimum 2.5 bar pressure), grease traps, gas manifolds, and floor drainage gullies to within 1.0 meter of designated equipment placement locations.
  • Site Clearances: Physical access routes, service elevators, and doorways must be clear of obstructions. Any craning, hoisting, or architectural door frame removal required for oversized equipment is the Client's operational responsibility.
  • Commissioning Sign-off: Upon installation, our certified engineering team will conduct live-fire and mechanical testing. The Client's designated executive chef or facility manager must sign the Commissioning Handover Protocol to validate warranty commencement.
Clause 07

Manufacturer Warranties & Service SLAs

Sip & Play stands behind every piece of commercial machinery with comprehensive manufacturer warranty backing and dedicated local technical teams:

Equipment Category Warranty Period Coverage Details
Hambach Cooking Ranges & Combi Ovens 12 Months Standard Comprehensive parts & on-site labor coverage against manufacturing defects
Commercial Refrigeration & Chillers 12 Mos Parts / 24 Mos Compressor Compressor failure, thermostat, and refrigeration circuit components
Espresso Machines & Beverage Systems 12 Months Standard Heating elements, boilers, rotary volumetric pumps, and control boards
Custom Stainless Steel Fabrication 36 Months Structural Weld integrity, SUS304 grade verification, and chassis stability

Warranty Exclusions: Warranties do not cover damage caused by electrical voltage surges/brownouts without an inline AVR/stabilizer, scale buildup from unsoftened water supplies (>50 ppm CaCO3), lack of routine grease filter cleaning, physical operator abuse, or repairs attempted by unauthorized third-party technicians.

Clause 08

Custom Fabrication, Cancellations & Returns

Due to the tailored nature of commercial food service architecture:

  • Bespoke Stainless Steel: Custom-dimensioned worktables, sink units, exhaust hoods, cold rooms, and fabricated counters cannot be cancelled or returned once metal cutting and welding has commenced at the factory.
  • Standard Stock Machinery: Cancellation of standard catalogue equipment prior to dispatch is subject to a 20% restocking and administrative fee.
  • Defective Deliveries: Any physical transit damage must be recorded on the delivery note and reported to Sip & Play in writing within forty-eight (48) hours of receipt, accompanied by photographic evidence.
Clause 09

Digital Client Portal Governance & Security

Clients granted access to the Sip & Play Client Portal agree to:

  • Maintain strict confidentiality of portal login credentials and promptly notify us of any unauthorized account access.
  • Use the portal solely for reviewing project CAD blueprints, equipment quotation schedules, shipping timelines, and submitting warranty service requests.
  • Not scrape, reverse-engineer, or attempt to compromise portal security architectures.
Clause 10

Limitation of Liability & Force Majeure

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law:

  • Liability Cap: Sip & Play's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with any project, whether in contract, tort (including negligence), or otherwise, shall be strictly capped at the total fees received by Sip & Play for the specific equipment or service giving rise to the claim.
  • Consequential Loss Exclusion: Under no circumstances shall Sip & Play be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential losses, including restaurant revenue loss, food spoilage during municipal power cuts, business interruption, or reputation damages.
  • Force Majeure: Neither party shall be held in breach for delays resulting from causes beyond reasonable control, including acts of God, maritime carrier congestion, customs embargoes, geopolitical strikes, typhoons, pandemics, or civil unrest.
Clause 11

Dispute Resolution & Governing Law

This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka:

  • Amicable Negotiation: The parties shall first attempt to resolve any disagreement through executive negotiation between senior directors within thirty (30) days.
  • Arbitration: If unresolved, disputes shall be finally settled under the Rules of Arbitration of the Sri Lanka Arbitration Act No. 11 of 1995 or the ICLP Arbitration Centre in Colombo, Sri Lanka, conducted in the English language.
  • Regional Enforcement: For projects in Maldives or Thailand, arbitral awards are mutually enforceable under the New York Convention on the Recognition and Enforcement of Foreign Arbitral Awards.
