04 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Stainless Steel Fabrication FAQs

Can Sip & Play fabricate L-shaped tables and custom sink assemblies around structural columns?

Yes. When executing 2D/3D Commercial Kitchen Design, our engineers laser-measure every column, alcove, and doorway to fabricate custom-fitted SUS304 tables, corner dish-pit inlet/outlet tables, and bar under-counters.

Why does Sip & Play specify AISI 304 instead of 201 stainless steel for food prep tables and sinks?

AISI 304 contains 18% chromium and 8%–10.5% nickel, resisting food acids, salt brines, and coastal humidity. Lower-cost 201-series steel replaces nickel with manganese and suffers weld pitting and rust in humid Sri Lankan and Maldivian kitchens.
