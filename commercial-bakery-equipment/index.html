04 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Commercial Bakery Equipment FAQs

What is the difference between a modular deck oven, a convection oven, and a rotary rack oven?

Modular deck ovens use radiant conduction from stone or steel hearths with independent top and bottom heat controls for artisan bread, pizza, and sponge cakes. Hot-air convection ovens circulate forced air across 4 to 10 trays for flaky croissants, puff pastries, and cookies. Roll-in rotary rack ovens rotate a full 16-, 32-, or 64-tray trolley inside a heated chamber for high-volume wholesale bread, bun, and biscuit commissaries.

Why do we need both a spiral mixer and a planetary mixer in a commercial bakery?

Planetary mixer gearboxes are engineered for high-RPM whipping and creaming of batters and frostings; kneading heavy, low-hydration bread dough in a planetary mixer causes premature gear wear. Dual-speed spiral mixers are specifically built to knead heavy bread, bun, and pizza doughs efficiently.

Should we choose LPG gas or 3-phase electric deck ovens in Sri Lanka?

LPG gas deck ovens consume minimal electrical power (typically under 200W per deck for digital controllers and ignition) and lower operating costs where 3-phase transformer capacity is limited. Three-phase (380V–400V) electric deck ovens offer precise solid-state top/bottom element modulation and are ideal for indoor shopping malls or hotel basements where gas piping is restricted.
