04 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Commercial Cooking Equipment FAQs

Should we choose LPG gas, 3-phase electric, or commercial induction for our hot line?

LPG gas ranges and woks deliver instant flame response independent of building electrical transformer limits, while 3-phase electric combi ovens and induction suites reduce kitchen ambient heat and are preferred in basement malls or overwater resorts where open flames are restricted. See our Commercial Kitchen Equipment Buying Guide.

What exhaust hood and gas safety interlocks are required for commercial cooking lines in Sri Lanka?

All grease-producing gas and electric cooking appliances require an AISI 304 Type-I exhaust canopy overhanging the cooking line by 150mm–300mm on open sides, equipped with 45° stainless baffle filters, 80%–85% make-up air replacement, and an emergency LPG gas solenoid valve interlocked with the exhaust fan.

Can Sip & Play supply a complete island cooking suite with matching under-counter chillers?

Yes. We engineer integrated hot-line suites combining ranges, fryers, griddles, custom SUS304 spreader tables, double overshelves, and opposite under-counter refrigerated chef drawers.

What equipment is needed for high-volume industrial catering of 1,000 to 6,000+ meals per day?

High-volume industrial commissaries replace small restaurant pans with 80L–150L motorized tilting bratt pans, 100L–300L jacketed steam kettles, 12-to-24-tray rice steaming cabinets, 20-tray GN 2/1 combi ovens, and multi-station high-pressure wok lines.
